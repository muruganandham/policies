Need help with Brick Classic? We’re here to help you get back to stacking blocks and chasing your high score.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Move the falling blocks left or right, rotate them, and complete full horizontal lines to clear space and increase your score.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Brick Classic can be played offline without an internet connection.

How is my high score saved?

Your high score is saved locally on your device.

I found a bug. What should I do?

Please email us with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.
