ColorStack

Stack colorful blocks, chase perfect timing, and build the tallest tower possible.

About ColorStack

ColorStack is a fun and addictive casual arcade game designed for quick play sessions. Tap at the right moment to stack moving blocks and create a colorful tower. The better your timing, the higher your score.

Features

  • Simple one-tap gameplay
  • Bright and colorful visuals
  • Endless arcade challenge
  • Perfect placement rewards
  • High score tracking
  • Optional rewarded ads to continue gameplay
  • No account required

How to Play

Tap to drop each moving block onto the stack. Align blocks perfectly to keep building higher. Misaligned blocks become smaller, making each move more challenging. Keep stacking and try to beat your best score!
