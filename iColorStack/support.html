Common Questions

Does ColorStack require an account?

No. ColorStack does not require login or account creation.

Does ColorStack work offline?

Yes. Core gameplay works offline. Internet may be required for rewarded advertisements.

Does ColorStack contain ads?

ColorStack may show optional rewarded ads, such as watching an ad to continue gameplay.
